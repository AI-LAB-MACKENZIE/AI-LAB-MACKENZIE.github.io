PPGA Interativo

A science communication project that uses artificial intelligence to turn doctoral theses and master's dissertations from the Business Management Graduate School into interactive presentations in plain language, bringing research results closer to managers, companies, students and society.

Flexibility Lens

A project that puts into practice the results of a doctoral thesis on cognitive flexibility in project teams. An interactive diagnostic, in Portuguese and English, shows managers and team members which criteria they use to judge flexibility, what their view tends to overlook and which profiles would round out the team.

Stable releasePresented as a PTT at EnANPAD 2026

GroupCognito

An agent-based simulation platform for studying group decisions. Each agent can be configured (personality, experience, access to information), the network can mimic a real or fictional organization, and the researcher follows how opinions spread and whether the group reaches the right decision. It runs batch experiments and has an AI experiment designer.

In developmentAn idea by Fellipe Silva Martins and Carlos Eduardo Pereira

Drills

An app for learning languages seriously, in a bootcamp format: the focus is on understanding and speaking, reaching the greatest autonomy in the shortest possible time. It starts with Japanese, with a Mandarin Chinese version developed with the support of Mackenzie's China Studies Center (NECH). It is in the stage before the minimum viable product (MVP).

FAHP-L

A Fuzzy AHP app for modeling strategic decisions with multiple criteria and alternatives. What is new is the adjustment by the respondents' hierarchical level (strategic, tactical or operational), with the traditional and adjusted results side by side. It checks the consistency of responses, generates charts and exports everything to Excel.

PrototypeBy Christian Falaster (FURB), with support from the lab

CVM DataFlow

An explorer for the financial data that publicly traded companies file with the CVM (Brazil's securities regulator): search by name or ticker, comparison between companies, financial ratios and data export. The software is by Christian Falaster, its lead author; the lab played a small supporting role.

Flexibility Lens

An interactive diagnostic for managers and members of project teams. Through a few pairwise choices, it shows the weight a person gives to four dimensions of cognitive flexibility, which of the seven profiles found in a study of 210 professionals is closest to their view, and who would complement that view on a team.
