Lab presents two works at EnANPAD 2026

← Back to news

The AI + Strategy Lab presented two works at EnANPAD 2026, the main meeting of the Associação Nacional de Pós-Graduação e Pesquisa em Administração (ANPAD). The meeting, which marked the association's 50th anniversary, took place from September 23 to 25, 2026, at UNIFOR in Fortaleza (CE).

Both works were presented in the Information Management (ATI) division and published in the ANPAD electronic proceedings.

A paper with an oral presentation

Humans or Machines? Evaluating the Capability of Large Language Models in Content Analysis for Management Research

The paper assesses how far large language models (LLMs) can carry out content analysis in management research, comparing the machines' work with that of researchers. It was presented in an oral session under the Impacts of Artificial Intelligence theme (ATI 5).

Authors: Carlos Eduardo Pereira, Suellen Miyahara Moraes Grum, Fellipe Silva Martins and Gilberto Perez, Business Management Graduate School (PPGA), Mackenzie Presbyterian University.

A technical-technological product

Agent-Based Simulation Platform for Analyzing Group Decision-Making Strategies

An agent-based simulation platform for analyzing group decision-making strategies. The work was presented as a technical-technological product (PTT), an ANPAD category for solutions that can be applied outside academia. Learn about the platform on the GroupCognito page.

Authors: Fellipe Silva Martins (PPGA, Mackenzie Presbyterian University) and Marcos Luiz Mucheroni (Graduate Program in Information Science, University of São Paulo).
