CVM DataFlow

An explorer for the financial data of Brazilian publicly traded companies.

← Back to Work

The project

CVM DataFlow downloads and organizes the annual financial statements (DFP) that publicly traded companies file with Brazil's Securities and Exchange Commission (CVM) and turns them into interactive dashboards. Users search for companies by name or ticker, choose the years and view a single company, compare up to five companies, track ratios such as liquidity, leverage and profitability, and export the raw data to spreadsheets. It is a tool for research and teaching in finance and strategy that saves the work of building these datasets by hand.

The software is by Christian Falaster (Regional University of Blumenau), lead author, who conceived, researched and developed the tool. The Lab, through Fellipe Silva Martins, played a small supporting role.

Innovation stage

CVM DataFlow is currently at stage 2: the app works as a prototype, with real CVM data. The goal is stage 3, with testing in research and in the classroom.
