The lab's first papers presented at ANPAD

← Back to news

The first papers produced with the support of the AI + Strategy Lab at Mackenzie Business School (Mackenzie Presbyterian University) were presented at the 3Es and EnATI divisional meetings of ANPAD (Associação Nacional de Pós-Graduação e Pesquisa em Administração).

Both meetings took place from June 9 to 11, 2026, at FGV EAESP in São Paulo. The papers address the interaction between artificial intelligence and strategic decisions:

AI Reliance as Strategic Behavior: A Behavioral Strategy Framework for Managing Dependence and Capability Erosion

3Es 2026, Behavioral Strategy theme (ESO 8), oral presentation. Author: Fellipe Silva Martins.

I don't know or I don't care: Epistemic and motivational drivers of frivolous use of generative AI

EnATI 2026, Impacts of Artificial Intelligence theme (ATI 5), oral presentation. Authors: Maria do Carmo Souto Rodriguez, Andrea Claudia Moras, Fellipe Silva Martins, Gilberto Perez and Claudio Luis Carvalho Larieira.
