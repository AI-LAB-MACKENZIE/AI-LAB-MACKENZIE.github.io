Lab presents Fuzzy AHP app at EnANPAD 2025

← Back to news

The AI + Strategy Lab presented its first work at EnANPAD 2025, the XLIX Encontro of the Associação Nacional de Pós-Graduação e Pesquisa em Administração (ANPAD). The meeting's theme was sustainability in management, and it took place from October 1 to 3, 2025, in Aracaju (SE). The work was published in the ANPAD electronic proceedings.

A technical-technological product

FAHP-L: A Level-Adjusted Fuzzy AHP App for Strategic Decision Modeling in Management Research

FAHP-L is an app for modeling strategic decisions with the Fuzzy AHP method, which organizes criteria and alternatives into a hierarchy and handles the uncertainty of decision-makers' judgments with fuzzy numbers. The work was presented as a technical-technological product (PTT), an ANPAD category for solutions that can be applied outside academia. Learn about the app on the FAHP-L page.

Author: Fellipe Silva Martins, Graduate Program in Business Administration (PPGA), Mackenzie Presbyterian University.
