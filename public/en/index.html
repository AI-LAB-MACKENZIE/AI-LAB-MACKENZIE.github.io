Artificial intelligence + strategy

We study how AI transforms decision-making, innovation and the behavior of organizations that operate in complex contexts.

Scroll to learn more ↓

The Lab

We bring together faculty, researchers and students to understand what changes (and what does not change) when machines start taking part in strategic decisions.
