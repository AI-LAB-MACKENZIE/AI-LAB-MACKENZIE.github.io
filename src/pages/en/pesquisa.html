What we do

What the Lab does

Research, science communication, teaching and the development of tools and products on artificial intelligence in organizations.

Lines of action

01

Research

Papers and technical products on AI, strategy and decision-making, presented and published at the field's leading meetings.

02

Science communication

Taking research beyond the university, in plain language, to managers, companies, students and society.

03

Teaching

Real cases and ready-to-use classroom materials for undergraduate, MBA and graduate courses, plus the training of researchers.

04

Tools and products

Software and apps born from research, and ideas that can become businesses, from the incubator to the market.

Research areas

All of the Lab's research starts from one question: what changes in organizational strategy when artificial intelligence starts taking part in decisions? It unfolds into five areas.

01Strategy02Decision-making03Innovation04Digital transformation05Organizational behavior
01

Strategy

How artificial intelligence redefines competitive advantage, firm scope and business models.

  • AI as a strategic resource
  • Data-driven business models
  • Strategy in complex contexts
02

Decision-making

Humans and algorithms deciding together: biases, trust, delegation and dependence on AI.

  • AI-augmented decision-making
  • AI dependence and the erosion of capabilities
  • Frivolous use of generative AI
03

Innovation

Generative AI as a driver of new products, services and processes.

  • Open innovation and ecosystems
  • Dynamic capabilities and AI
04

Digital transformation

How organizations adopt, adapt and scale AI technologies.

  • AI adoption in organizations
  • Digital platforms
05

Organizational behavior

Work, leadership and teams when AI becomes part of the routine.

  • Collaboration between people and AI
  • Leadership and culture
