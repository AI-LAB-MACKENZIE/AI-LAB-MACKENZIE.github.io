The Lab joins Google's Antigravity for Science

← Back to news

The AI + Strategy Lab has been granted access through Google's program that gives scientists free access to its most advanced AI models in Google Antigravity. The application was made on behalf of the Lab, with detailed information about its research and projects, and the access is tied to the account of its principal investigator, Fellipe Silva Martins. The program opened applications on October 13, 2026, with 10,000 places for researchers, and offers six months at the highest usage tier, including the Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 4 Carbon models. Access is valid through May 24, 2027.

We are really glad to receive this support. We hope to make full use of these models over the next six months, in the lab's research and tools.
