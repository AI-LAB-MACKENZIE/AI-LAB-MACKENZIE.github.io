Lab of AI for Strategy and Decision-Making approved with funding from MackPesquisa

← Back to news

Mackenzie Presbyterian University has approved the project to create AI Lab Mackenzie, fully funded by the Mackenzie Research Fund (MackPesquisa).

The lab is in its implementation and formalization phase and is part of Mackenzie Business School.

The initiative brings together a team of researchers made up of faculty members and students of Mackenzie Presbyterian University, along with collaborators from other institutions, focused on applied research in artificial intelligence in management and strategy.

Location

AI Lab Mackenzie is located at Rua Maria Antônia, 163, Room 13, Higienópolis, São Paulo (SP).
